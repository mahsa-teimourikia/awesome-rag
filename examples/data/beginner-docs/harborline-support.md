# Harborline Support Handbook

## Incident updates

When a customer reports a checkout error, support must first check the public service-status page and the incident queue. Do not promise a recovery time until an incident commander publishes one.

## Customer communication

For a confirmed payment incident, acknowledge the report, include the incident identifier, and give the customer the status-page URL. Enterprise customers receive an update every 30 minutes while an incident is open.

## Escalation boundary

Support can create a ticket and collect logs, but it cannot restart production services. A production action requires incident-command approval and a recorded change request.
