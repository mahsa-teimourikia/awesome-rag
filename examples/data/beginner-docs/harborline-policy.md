# Harborline Knowledge Policy

## Evidence standard

An answer must distinguish an observed fact from a recommendation. Every factual statement should be traceable to an indexed source and stable chunk identifier.

## Unknowns

If the corpus does not contain enough evidence, say what is unknown and suggest the next safe verification step. Do not infer an incident, a policy exception, or a customer entitlement from a partial keyword match.

## Source freshness

For operational answers, show the source name and version or retrieval time. A citation proves where text came from; it does not by itself prove that the text is current or authorized.
