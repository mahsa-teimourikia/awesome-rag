# Deal Discounting Policy
Effective Date: 2025-05-15
Version: 1.1
Status: Active

## 1. Standard Discounts
Account Executives are pre-authorized to offer up to a 10% discount on standard list pricing to close a deal by the end of the quarter.

## 2. Manager Approval
Discounts between 11% and 20% require written approval from the Regional Sales Manager in Salesforce before the quote can be sent to the customer.

## 3. VP Approval
Any discount exceeding 20% must be approved by the VP of Sales. These are strictly reserved for strategic accounts or competitive replacements.

## 4. Exceptions
Startups (companies under 50 employees and less than $5M in funding) automatically qualify for the "Northstar Startup Tier," which provides a flat 50% discount on the first year. This does not require Manager or VP approval.
