# Enterprise Sales Commission Structure
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Version: 2026.1
Status: Active

## 1. Base Quota and Rates
Account Executives (AEs) are assigned a quarterly quota. 
- **Tier 1 (0% - 100% of Quota):** AEs earn 10% commission on the Annual Contract Value (ACV) of closed-won deals.
- **Tier 2 (101% - 150% of Quota):** AEs earn an accelerated rate of 15% on the ACV for all deals closed above the 100% threshold.
- **Tier 3 (150%+ of Quota):** AEs earn a hyper-accelerated rate of 20% on the ACV for all deals closed above the 150% threshold.

## 2. Multi-Year Contracts
If a customer signs a multi-year contract (e.g., 3 years) upfront, the AE receives the standard commission on the Year 1 ACV, plus a 5% bonus on the Year 2 and Year 3 ACV, paid out immediately upon contract signature.

## 3. Clawbacks
If a customer churns or breaks their contract within the first 6 months, 100% of the commission paid for that deal will be clawed back from the AE's subsequent commission checks.
