# Data Retention Policy
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Version: 1.2
Status: Active

## 1. Overview
This policy defines how long Northstar Technologies retains different types of corporate and customer data to ensure compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and internal audit requirements.

## 2. Retention Periods

### 2.1 Customer Data
- **Active Accounts:** Retained indefinitely while the account is active.
- **Closed Accounts (Standard):** Retained for 30 days post-closure, then permanently purged from production databases.
- **Closed Accounts (Enterprise):** Retained for 90 days post-closure to allow for contract renegotiation, unless explicitly requested for immediate deletion by the customer.

### 2.2 Financial Records
- All invoices, billing records, and tax documents must be retained for exactly 7 years from the date of the transaction.
- In the event of an active audit, related financial records are placed on Legal Hold and cannot be deleted until the audit officially closes.

### 2.3 Internal Communications
- **Slack/Teams Messages:** Automatically purged after 90 days.
- **Email:** Retained for 1 year. Emails older than 1 year are archived in Cold Storage.

## 3. Data Deletion Protocol
Data deletion requests (e.g., GDPR Right to be Forgotten) must be executed within 14 days of receipt. The deletion must cover all primary databases, replicas, and backups.
