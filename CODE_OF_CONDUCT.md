# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Awesome RAG a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- communicating with empathy, patience, and respect;
- giving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- welcoming questions and different levels of experience;
- crediting sources and other contributors;
- respecting privacy, security boundaries, and requests to stop; and
- focusing disagreements on ideas and evidence rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, or publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project events.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. They may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact methods on [@mahsa-teimourikia's GitHub profile](https://github.com/mahsa-teimourikia). Do not open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers will disclose conflicts of interest and ask another appropriate reviewer to handle a report when necessary.
